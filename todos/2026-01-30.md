**Subject:** Volunteer Computational Research Assistant / Developer – HBC Lab

HBC Lab is seeking a **Computational Research Assistant / Developer** to support ongoing neuroscience research projects. This role is ideal for an undergraduate student interested in gaining hands-on experience working with real multimodal research data and research software systems.

This position will initially be **volunteer-based**, with the intention of transitioning 1–2 strong contributors into a **grant-funded paid Research Assistant role** starting this fall.

---

### Role Overview

You will contribute to data collection, quality assurance, and analysis across multiple research projects involving:

- fMRI
    
- Genetics
    
- Accelerometer data
    
- Cognitive and behavioral assessments
    

The work is highly technical and focuses on building and maintaining the infrastructure that supports large-scale research workflows.

---

### Logistics

- **Role:** Computational Research Assistant / Developer
    
- **Location:** Hybrid (in-person work is strongly encouraged)
    
- **Time Commitment:** ~6–10 hours per week
    
- **Compensation:** Volunteer initially; potential paid role in fall
    

---

### Technical Scope

You may work on:

- Data engineering pipelines
    
- Research automation and DevOps tooling
    
- Visualization and reporting systems
    
- Full-stack internal research tools
    

**Tech stack includes:**  
Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Bash, Git, GitHub Actions (CI/CD), Docker, Linux, and HPC environments.

---

### Candidate Profile

**Target level:** Undergraduate

**Must-have:**

- Proficiency in at least one relevant programming language
    
- Strong communication and reliability
    
- Willingness to work on technically complex systems
    

**Nice-to-have:**

- Experience with Linux
    
- Experience working in team-based software projects
    
- Willingness to commit additional hours when needed
    

---

### Growth Opportunities

Strong contributors will receive:

- Co-authorship on publications
    
- Conference submission opportunities
    
- Letters of recommendation
    
- Direct mentorship in computational neuroscience
    

Top candidates may be selected for **grant-funded paid RA positions** beginning this summer.

---

### Who This Is For

This role is best suited for **driven, self-motivated students** who want to build real systems used in active research. It is not a good fit for students looking for passive or low-effort involvement.
