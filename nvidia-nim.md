## What is NVIDIA AI Enterprise (NVAIE)
---
***Pure Definition*** - NVAIE is a suite of tools and frameworks designed for businesses to take AI from prototype to production. It solves scalability and infrastructure compatibility issues through a closed-source software suite which optimizes inference for AI on Nvidia hardware.

#### NVAIE Works on 2 Primary Layers
##### Application Layer
---
**The application layer focuses on SDKs, tools, and frameworks for optimized deployment and inference speed on the application/model side**
- These are all built on CUDA and CUDAx

##### Infrastructure Layer
---
**Provides a suite of optimized drivers for running inference on bare metal and cloud environments**
- These are all built on the underlying Nvidia GPU drivers
