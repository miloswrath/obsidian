
## OBSIDIAN ARTIFACT CO

**About the business**  
I run a small e-commerce company selling **premium, themed physical products** (think niche but giftable — not mass-market junk). We sell **direct-to-consumer**, mostly to people who already have an interest in the theme and are willing to pay more for quality and presentation.

**Who we sell to**

- Adults 25–45
    
- Online shoppers who value **aesthetic, story, and credibility**, not just price
    
- A lot of first-time buyers come from social media or referrals
    
- Many purchases are gifts, so trust and clarity matter a lot
    

**What we sell**

- A small catalog (not hundreds of SKUs)
    
- Products are visually distinctive
    
- Margins depend heavily on branding and perceived quality, not volume
    

**What customers want from us**

- Confidence the product is _legit_ and worth the price
    
- Clear explanation of _what makes it different_
    
- Fast understanding of shipping, returns, and support
    
- Reassurance they won’t get scammed or disappointed
    

If a customer has to “dig” to understand the value, we lose them.

**How this should reflect in the website**

- Strong, clean homepage that explains the value in **5–10 seconds**
    
- High-quality visuals front and center (bad visuals would kill us)
    
- Simple navigation — I hate clutter and so do customers
    
- Product pages that answer questions _before_ the customer asks them
    
- Clear trust signals (policies, reviews, real brand voice)
    

**What I want out of the website**

- It should **sell without me explaining things**
    
- It needs to feel credible, modern, and intentional
    
- Easy for non-technical people (like me) to update basics
    
- Fast loading — I don’t care how fancy it is if it’s slow
    
- Designed to scale later, not rebuilt from scratch in a year
    

**What I _don’t_ want**

- Over-engineered tech I don’t understand
    
- Generic templates that look like everyone else
    
- Long walls of text pretending to be “branding”
    
- A site that looks good but doesn’t convert
