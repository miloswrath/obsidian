
# Humanities Based Aging Neuroscience Intervention
---

## Breakdown
---
**Problem:** Older adults experiencing mild cognitive impairment often struggle with episodic recall and identity continuity.  

**Humanities Intervention:** Guided life-story writing or digital storytelling sessions that cue autobiographical memory and strengthen narrative identity.  

**Why it works (neuroscience tie-in):** Autobiographical narrative activates hippocampal–prefrontal circuits; repeated narrative structuring supports compensatory cognitive strategies.

## Problem Specification
---
- Neurodegeneration schemas typical with aging directly impact the complex associated processes and systems that contribute to autobiographical memory performance [paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00429-005-0038-0)
- Lackluster AM performance contributes to accelerated loss and behavioral changes within older adults, worsening quality of life.
