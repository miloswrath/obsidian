## Contrast
- difference between elements that make them stand apart

## Balance
- distribution of visual weight of objects colors and space

## Emphasis
- certain parts stand out compared to other (or to minimize)

## Proportion
- size in relation
- larger is seen as more vice versa

## Hierarchy
- importance of elements
- elements should read by importance by visual intuition

## Repetition
- Reusing elements (colors, fonts, images) to reinforce an idea

## Rhythm
- Spacing used to elicit emotion 
- regular spacing to form calmness or regularity
- irregular is more ecstatic

## Pattern
- repetition of design elements or standards for how certain elements are designed

## White space
- AKA negative space 
- areas without design - important for reducing overload and emphasis of elements

## Movement
- movement of audiences eyes
- designing around moving from each element to the next - position size etc.

## Variety
- creates visual interest
- mixing typography color images textures etc to prevent monotony

## Unity
- how well the elements fit together
- clear visual relationships with another in order to have clear and concise message
