## Terms
- *Population* - entire class of people studying
- *sample* - the subset of population who data was collected on
- *inference* - researchers apply theory and reason to explain relationships in data
- *parameters* - numbers that describe the whole population
	- estimated from the sample
- *statistic* - number computed from sample
- *bias* - centered around truth of population?
- *variability* - confidence in estimate

## Main questions
- how do i collect the data?
- how do i describe the data in my sample?
- what does this tell me about general population
