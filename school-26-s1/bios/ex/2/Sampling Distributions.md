## Sampling Distributions
---
![[Pasted image 20251017093649.png]]
> We have a sample, how do we make an inference or generalization about the population?

### Terminology
---
$\mu$ - population mean
$\bar{X}$ - sample mean
$\cfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$ - Standard Error (variation of the sample means)

## Central Limit Theorem
---
> As sample size $n$ becomes larger, the distribution of $\bar{X}$ becomes approximately normal. This is especially true at around 30

*This only applies to sample means, not other statistics*
### Calculations From This
---
If we have a large enough $n$ we can then say that $\bar{X} \approx N(\mu, \cfrac{\sigma^2}{\sqrt{n}})$
